Frequently Asked Questions

How long should I plan to live in a house to make buying worthwhile?

The general rule of thumb in the US is at least 4 to 5 years. This gives the property enough time to appreciate and offset the 3% buyer closing costs and the 6% agent commission when you eventually sell.

What costs do renters avoid that owners pay?

Homeowners are on the hook for property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA fees, and routine maintenance (HVAC replacements, roof repairs, plumbing), which typically averages 1% to 1.5% of the home value annually.

Does this account for the mortgage interest deduction?

Most US homeowners now take the standard deduction ($14,600 single / $29,200 married filing jointly) following the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, meaning mortgage interest only provides tax savings if total itemized deductions exceed the standard threshold.
